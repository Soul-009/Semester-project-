Hero Guide

Meet the heroes currently in the roster, from frontline anchors to quick-moving damage dealers.

D.Va Genji Mercy Reinhardt Tracer Winston

TANK · BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA

D.Va

Hana Song

Former professional gamer turned MEKA pilot, D.Va protects her home by taking the fight directly to the enemy. Her mech can absorb incoming fire, boost into combat, and self-destruct when the moment is right.

Signature abilities

  • Defense Matrix
  • Boosters
  • Micro Missiles
  • Self-Destruct

DAMAGE · SHIMADA CLAN

Genji

Genji Shimada

Rescued and rebuilt after a conflict with his family, Genji found a new balance between his human and cyborg sides. He uses precision, mobility, and a blade to strike before opponents can react.

Signature abilities

  • Shuriken
  • Deflect
  • Swift Strike
  • Dragonblade

SUPPORT · SWITZERLAND

Mercy

Dr. Angela Ziegler

A gifted doctor and field medic, Mercy is devoted to saving lives. She moves between teammates to heal them, amplify their damage, and bring a fallen ally back into the fight.

Signature abilities

  • Caduceus Staff
  • Guardian Angel
  • Resurrect
  • Valkyrie

TANK · STUTTGART, GERMANY

Reinhardt

Reinhardt Wilhelm

A courageous knight in powered armor, Reinhardt leads from the front. His barrier shields the team as it advances, while his rocket hammer and powerful charge punish anyone who gets too close.

Signature abilities

  • Rocket Hammer
  • Barrier Field
  • Charge
  • Earthshatter

DAMAGE · LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM

Tracer

Lena Oxton

An adventurous former pilot, Tracer can leap through time in short bursts. Her speed lets her harass isolated opponents, then rewind to recover and escape before they can respond.

Signature abilities

  • Pulse Pistols
  • Blink
  • Recall
  • Pulse Bomb

TANK · HORIZON LUNAR COLONY

Winston

Winston

A scientist and steadfast Overwatch agent, Winston leaps into the back line to disrupt opponents and protect his team. His Tesla Cannon arcs electricity across nearby enemies.

Signature abilities

  • Tesla Cannon
  • Jump Pack
  • Barrier Projector
  • Primal Rage
